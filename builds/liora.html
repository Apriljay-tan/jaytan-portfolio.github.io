LIORA SDA

Studying meant jumping from a Bible app to the Sabbath School lesson and then the hymnal. LIORA, "My Light," keeps them in one pocket: King James, Cebuano, and NLT, a quarterly lesson that fetches the new one, and a hymnal in English and Cebuano. It works offline.
